1. "4X4": 
    +4 points for each dish you ate exactly 4 tiles from.
    +4 points if you tasted exactly 4 dish types.
2. "Tahini party":
    +1 point for each two tahini tokens you consumed.
3. "Sesame intolerance":
    +3*(number_of_players-1) points if you consumed less tahini tokens
    than any other player, or consumed no tahini at all.
4. "Tahini queen":
    +2*(number_of_players-1) points if you consumed more tahini tokens 
    than any other player.
5. "Tasting menu":
    +5 points if you tasted exactly 5 dish types.
    +6 points if you tasted exactly 6 dish types.
    +7 points if you tasted exactly 7 dish types.
    These points are on top of the standard bonuses.
6. "picky eater":
    +15 points if you tasted exactly 3 dish types.
7. "Some like it hot":
    +1 for each 2 hot dish tile consumed.
8. "Too hot to handle":
    +3*(number_of_players-1) points if you consumed more hot dish tiles 
    than any other player, including extra-hot berbere.
9. "Beetroot boss":
    +2 points for each key sir tile consumed.
10. "No hot for you!":
    +3*(number_of_players-1) points if you consumed no hot dish tiles,
    or fewer hot dish tiles than every other player.
11. "Peas please":
    +1 point for every dish tile consumed containing peas (Shiro and Kik Alicha)
12. "Peas prince":
    +2*(number_of_players-1) points if you consumed more tiles with peas
    (Shiro and Kik Alicha) than any other player.
13. "lentils freak":
    +1 point for every dish tile consumed containing lentils (Misir Wot and Azifa).
14. "lentils princess":
    +2*(number_of_players-1) points if you consumed more tiles with lentils
    (Misir Wot and Azifa) than any other player.
15. "Cabbage savage":
    +1 point for every dish tile consumed containing cabbage (Gomen and Tikel Gomen).
16. "Cabbage king":
    +2*(number_of_players-1) points if you consumed more tiles with cabbage
    (Gomen and Tikel Gomen) than any other player.
17: "Healthy appetite":
    +2*(number_of_players-1) if you ate more dish tiles than any other player.
18: "Consolation prize":
    +3*(number_of_players-1) if you ate less dish tiles than any other player.
19: "Delicate palate":
    +1 for each 2 non-hot dish tile consumed.
